LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This AGREEMENT is made at Mumbai on this 20th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. BALWANT R. SINGHANIA, Residing at 14, Silver Arch, Perry Cross Road, Bandra (West), Mumbai - 400050 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR");

AND

MR. TANMAY G. CHITALE, Residing permanently at 202, Shradha Sadan, Tilak Road, Pune - 411030 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE").

WHEREAS the Licensor is the owner of Flat No. 1102, 11th Floor, 'Skyline Tower', Turner Road, Bandra (West), Mumbai - 400050 (the "PREMISES"), and the Licensee desires to take the Premises on leave and licence on the terms recorded below:

1. TERM AND IRREVOCABLE LOCK-IN:
The licence is granted for a term of 36 (thirty-six) months. Both parties agree that there shall be an absolute and irrevocable 24-month lock-in period. The Licensee shall have no right whatsoever to terminate this agreement or vacate the Premises during the said 24 months. If the Licensee abandons or vacates the Premises before 24 months, the Licensee shall remain strictly liable to pay the full monthly compensation for the entire remainder of the 24-month lock-in period as pre-estimated liquidated damages.

2. COMPENSATION, PENALTY, AND AGGRESSIVE ESCALATION:
The monthly compensation shall be Rs. 65,000/- (Rupees Sixty-Five Thousand only) payable strictly in advance on the 1st of each month. In case of any payment delay, a punitive late charge of Rs. 1,000/- per day shall be levied immediately. There shall be a mandatory 15% rent escalation every 11 months during the tenure.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND TOTAL FORFEITURE CLAUSE:
The Licensee has deposited a sum of Rs. 3,50,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs Fifty Thousand only) as security deposit. The said security deposit shall stand completely forfeited by the Licensor in the event of early termination, dispute, or minor infraction of society rules. Under normal termination, the deposit shall be refundable only after 120 days from surrender of possession, solely at the subjective discretion and satisfaction of the Licensor.

4. TAXES, SOCIETY CAPITAL OUTGOINGS, AND STRUCTURAL EXPENSES:
The Licensee shall be personally responsible for paying all municipal property tax increments, society capital sinking funds, major building overhaul levies, elevator repair contributions, and all building structural repairs, dampness restorations, and plumbing overhauls occurring during the licence term.

5. UNRESTRICTED ACCESS AND MASTER KEYS:
The Licensor and his agents retain master duplicate keys and shall have the unreserved right to enter and access the Premises at any hour of the day or night without prior notice, permission, or consent of the Licensee.

6. SUMMARY EVICTION AND CANCELLATION:
The Licensor reserves the unilateral right to cancel this licence and evict the Licensee upon giving 48 (forty-eight) hours' written notice without assigning any reason or justification, and the Licensee expressly waives all rights to claim compensation or approach any civil court.

7. INDEMNITY AND WAIVER OF DISPUTES:
The Licensee waives all legal remedies, disputes, and counterclaims against the Licensor. Any dispute shall be decided solely by an arbitrator appointed exclusively by the Licensor whose decision shall be final.
