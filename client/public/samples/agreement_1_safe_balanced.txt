This LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is made and entered into at Pune on this 5th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. RAJESH M. DESHMUKH, Age: 48 years, Occupation: Service, Residing at Flat No. 401, 'Aura Residency', Baner Road, Pune - 411045, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR" (which expression shall unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof include his legal heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND

MS. ANANYA SEN, Age: 29 years, Occupation: Software Engineer, Residing permanently at 14/B, Lake View Enclave, Salt Lake, Kolkata - 700091, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE" (which expression shall unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof include her heirs, executors, administrators, and permitted assigns) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS:
A. The Licensor is the sole and absolute owner of residential Flat No. 302, 3rd Floor, Building-C, 'Green Meadows', Baner-Pashan Link Road, Pune - 411021, comprising 2 Bedrooms, Hall, Kitchen, with attached balconies and one covered car parking space (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSED PREMISES").
B. The Licensee has approached the Licensor with a request to grant leave and licence to occupy the Licensed Premises solely for residential purposes for herself on temporary basis.
C. The Licensor has agreed to grant leave and licence on mutual terms and covenants set forth herein below.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IS HEREBY MUTUALLY AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:

1. GRANT AND PURPOSE:
The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee temporary leave and licence to occupy and use the Licensed Premises strictly for residential purposes. The Licensee shall not use the premises for any commercial, illegal, or unethical purposes.

2. PERIOD OF LICENCE:
The duration of this licence shall be for a fixed term of 11 (eleven) months, commencing from 15th October 2026 and expiring on 14th September 2027.

3. COMPENSATION / MONTHLY LICENCE FEE:
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly compensation of Rs. 28,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only) per English calendar month. The said compensation shall be paid on or before the 7th day of each calendar month directly into the bank account of the Licensor. There shall be no escalation in monthly compensation during the 11-month term of this agreement.

4. REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 56,000/- (Rupees Fifty-Six Thousand only), equivalent to two months' compensation. The said security deposit shall be refunded in full by electronic transfer to the Licensee within 7 (seven) business days of peaceful handover of the vacant premises, subject only to deductions for actual unpaid electricity or utility bills and verifiable physical damages beyond fair wear and tear, as assessed during a joint exit inspection.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND TERMINATION NOTICE:
Both parties agree to a mutual lock-in period of 3 (three) months from the commencement date. Following the expiry of the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement at any time by serving 1 (one) month's advance written notice or by paying 1 month's compensation in lieu thereof. Neither party shall be liable for any additional financial penalty upon valid notice.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS:
The Licensor shall be responsible for all society maintenance charges, municipal property taxes, non-occupancy fees, and all major or structural repairs, including external wall seepages, plumbing pipeline faults, and roof leakages. The Licensee shall be responsible for routine day-to-day minor repairs and replacement of consumables (such as light bulbs and tap washers) up to an amount of Rs. 500/- per instance.

7. INSPECTION AND ACCESS:
The Licensor or his designated representative shall have the right to inspect the Licensed Premises only upon giving at least 24 (twenty-four) hours' prior written notice to the Licensee. Such inspections shall strictly take place during reasonable daytime hours between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM, with due respect to the Licensee's privacy.

8. REGISTRATION, STAMP DUTY, AND POLICE VERIFICATION:
The cost of stamp duty and government registration fees for this agreement shall be borne equally (50:50) by both the Licensor and the Licensee. The Licensor and Licensee shall jointly complete the mandatory tenant police verification with the local jurisdiction police station within 15 days from the execution of this agreement.

9. PEACEFUL SURRENDER:
Upon the expiry or sooner termination of this licence, the Licensee shall peacefully hand over vacant possession of the premises in the same state as received, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands to this agreement on the date first written above.
