LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This AGREEMENT is executed at Pune on this 12th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. PRADEEP S. JOSHI, residing at Plot No. 18, Mayur Colony, Kothrud, Pune - 411038 (the "LICENSOR");

AND

MR. ROHAN VERMA, permanent resident of Flat 3, Shanti Nagar, Nashik - 422002 (the "LICENSEE").

WHEREAS the Licensor has agreed to license Flat No. 102, 'Siddhi Heights', Paud Road, Kothrud, Pune - 411038 to the Licensee for residential purposes on the following terms:

1. PERIOD:
The licence is granted for an 11-month period from 1st November 2026 to 30th September 2027.

2. COMPENSATION:
Monthly licence fee of Rs. 26,000/- shall be payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every calendar month. If delayed beyond 5th, a late fee of Rs. 200 per day shall be charged.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT & REFUND TIMELINE:
The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh only). The deposit shall be refunded within 45 (forty-five) days after the Licensee vacates and surrenders the keys, subject to deductions for any pending utility dues and flat touch-ups.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND PENALTY:
There shall be an agreed lock-in period of 9 (nine) months from the commencement date. If the Licensee terminates the agreement or vacates before completing 9 months, the Licensee shall pay a premature exit penalty of 1 (one) month's compensation to the Licensor.

5. TERMINATION NOTICE:
After completion of the 9-month lock-in period, the Licensee must provide 2 (two) months' prior written notice to terminate the agreement. The Licensor may terminate the agreement at any time by giving 1 (one) month's written notice to the Licensee.

6. MAINTENANCE AND INTERNAL REPAIRS:
The Licensee shall bear the cost of all minor repairs up to Rs. 2,500/- per occasion, and shall also contribute 50% of the cost for any internal plumbing or electrical rewiring required during the licence period. The Licensor shall pay standard monthly society maintenance bills.

7. INSPECTION:
The Licensor may visit and inspect the premises after providing a short 2 (two) hours' verbal intimation over phone call during normal daytime hours.

8. POLICE VERIFICATION:
The Licensee shall provide necessary identification documents to complete tenant police verification at the Kothrud police station within 15 days of occupancy.
