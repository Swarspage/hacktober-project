LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is made at Pune this 10th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MRS. SUNITA K. KULKARNI, Indian Inhabitant, residing at B-502, Nyati Empire, Kharadi, Pune - 411014, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR" (which term shall include her successors and legal representatives) of the ONE PART;

AND

MR. VIKRAM MALHOTRA, Indian Inhabitant, working with Infosys Ltd., residing permanently at House No. 221, Sector 15, Chandigarh - 160015, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE" (which term shall include his legal heirs and administrators) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor owns residential premises Flat No. 204, Second Floor, 'Hermes Heritage', Shastri Nagar, Yerawada, Pune - 411006 (the "PREMISES"), and the Licensee desires to take the Premises on temporary leave and licence for residential use.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. DURATION:
The Licence is granted for a total tenure of 22 (twenty-two) months commencing from 1st November 2026 and expiring on 31st August 2028.

2. LICENCE FEE AND ESCALATION:
The Licensee shall pay monthly compensation of Rs. 35,000/- (Thirty-Five Thousand Rupees only) for the first 11 months. Effective from the 12th month, the monthly licence fee shall be revised upwards by a reasonable 5% (five percent), making the fee Rs. 36,750/- per month for the remaining tenure. Payment shall be remitted on or before the 10th day of every calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT & REFUND SCHEDULE:
The Licensee has remitted an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 70,000/- (Rupees Seventy Thousand only) to the Licensor. The Licensor covenants to refund the said deposit in full within 15 (fifteen) calendar days from the date the Licensee vacates the Premises. Any proposed deduction must be substantiated by written receipts or actual utility bills shared with the Licensee prior to deduction.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND MUTUAL EXIT:
A initial lock-in period of 6 (six) months shall apply to both parties. After the expiry of 6 months, either party may terminate this agreement by providing 1 (one) month's written notice or compensation in lieu thereof. Neither party shall levy any additional penalty upon proper service of notice.

5. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE DIVISION:
The Licensor shall be responsible for all society maintenance charges, capital sinking fund contributions, property taxes, and structural repairs (including plumbing lines within walls, structural dampness, and building defects). The Licensee shall maintain the interior fixtures in good working condition and attend to minor consumable replacements (fuses, tap washers, bulbs).

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION PROTOCOL:
The Licensor or authorized representatives may visit the Premises for inspection only by giving 48 (forty-eight) hours' prior notice in writing or via email to the Licensee. All visits shall be scheduled on weekends between 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM to avoid disrupting the Licensee's personal schedule.

7. REGISTRATION AND STATUTORY COMPLIANCE:
The registration of this agreement under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 shall be completed online, with the registration charges and stamp duty shared in equal 50:50 proportion. The Licensor shall facilitate the police verification process within 30 days of moving in.

8. VACATING PREMISES:
The Licensee shall peacefully surrender the Premises upon termination of the agreement, normal wear and tear and damage by natural elements excepted.
