LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is made and executed at Mumbai on this 15th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. A SHARMA, an adult Indian inhabitant, residing at 402, Sea Crest Apartments, Juhu Tara Road, Mumbai - 400049, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR" (which expression shall unless it be repugnant to the context or meaning thereof mean and include his legal heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND

MS. R PATIL, an adult Indian inhabitant, having permanent residence at Flat No. 12, Swapnapurti Colony, Kothrud, Pune - 411038, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE" (which expression shall unless it be repugnant to the context or meaning thereof mean and include her heirs, executors, and administrators) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS:
A. The Licensor is the absolute lawful owner and in peaceful possession of residential Flat No. 602, 6th Floor, A-Wing, Palm Grove Co-operative Housing Society Ltd., Off New Link Road, Andheri (West), Mumbai - 400053, comprising 2 BHK along with fixtures and fittings (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSED PREMISES").
B. The Licensee has approached the Licensor with a request to grant leave and licence to occupy and use the Licensed Premises strictly for residential purposes for herself.
C. Relying upon the representations made by the Licensee, the Licensor has agreed to grant temporary Leave and Licence to use and occupy the Licensed Premises on the terms and conditions hereinafter appearing.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY MUTUALLY AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:

1. GRANT OF LICENCE:
The Licensor hereby grants unto the Licensee the temporary permission, leave, and licence to occupy and use the Licensed Premises solely for residential use, and for no other commercial or illegal purpose whatsoever.

2. TERM OF LICENCE:
The licence is granted for an initial duration of 24 (twenty-four) months, commencing from 1st November 2026 and expiring on 31st October 2028.

3. COMPENSATION / MONTHLY LICENCE FEE & ESCALATION:
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly compensation of Rs. 45,000/- (Rupees Forty-Five Thousand only) in advance on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month by electronic bank transfer. The parties agree that there shall be an automatic 10% annual rent escalation on the monthly license fee upon the completion of every 12 months of occupancy.

4. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor a sum of Rs. 1,80,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Eighty Thousand only) as an interest-free refundable security deposit. The said security deposit shall be refunded after owner's satisfaction regarding the internal condition, paint, and cleanliness of the flat upon the Licensee vacating the premises, with no fixed deadline or stipulated timeframe for the Licensor to process the refund. The Licensor reserves the unilateral right to deduct any sums deemed fit for restoration.

5. MANDATORY LOCK-IN PERIOD & PENALTY:
Both parties explicitly agree to a strict mandatory 12-month lock-in period commencing from the date of possession. The Licensee shall not be entitled to terminate this agreement or vacate the Licensed Premises during this lock-in period. If the Licensee chooses to vacate or terminate this licence prior to the completion of the 12-month lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay a premature exit penalty equivalent to 3 months' rent, and the Licensor shall be entitled to deduct the same from the security deposit or recover it as arrears.

6. NOTICE PERIOD:
Subject to the expiration of the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by serving 1 (one) month's prior notice in writing to the other party.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS:
The Licensee accepts the Licensed Premises in its present condition. The Licensee expressly agrees that all repairs charged to the licensee without exception, including all day-to-day minor repairs, electrical faults, plumbing, drainage blockages, seepage, sanitary replacements, as well as all major and structural repairs to walls, beams, ceilings, society pipelines, or building infrastructure, shall be carried out and paid for exclusively by the Licensee at her own cost.

8. INSPECTION AND ENTRY:
The Licensor, his family members, representatives, and authorized brokers shall be entitled to enter the Licensed Premises at any time of the day or night without prior notice to the Licensee, for the purpose of inspection, viewing the maintenance, or showing the premises to prospective purchasers or incoming licensees.

9. RESTRICTIONS ON USE AND SUBLETTING:
The Licensee shall not sublet, assign, transfer, or part with possession of the Licensed Premises or any portion thereof to any third party under any circumstances. The premises shall not be used for paying guests, bachelor accommodation, or commercial business activities.

10. COMPLIANCE WITH SOCIETY BYE-LAWS:
The Licensee shall abide by all rules, bye-laws, and regulations of the Palm Grove Co-operative Housing Society Ltd. The monthly society maintenance charges of Rs. 4,500/- shall be paid directly by the Licensee in addition to the monthly compensation.

11. INDEMNITY:
The Licensee shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Licensor against all claims, damages, liabilities, fines, or losses arising out of any breach of covenant or negligence committed by the Licensee or her visitors.

12. VACATING UPON TERMINATION:
Upon expiration or earlier determination of this licence, the Licensee shall peacefully vacate and hand over the key and vacant possession of the Licensed Premises to the Licensor. Failure to vacate shall make the Licensee liable to pay damages at Rs. 3,000/- per day until actual physical possession is restored.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Licensor and the Licensee have signed and executed this Leave and Licence Agreement on the day, month, and year first hereinabove written.
