LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is executed at Thane on this 18th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. NITIN G. MEHTA, Residing at 703, Blue Horizon, Ghodbunder Road, Thane (West) - 400607 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR");

AND

MS. NEHA S. IYER, Residing permanently at Flat 104, Gokul Dham, Alwarpet, Chennai - 600018 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE").

WHEREAS the Licensor has agreed to license Flat No. 402, B-Wing, 'Regency Park', Pokhran Road No. 2, Thane (West) - 400610 (the "PREMISES") to the Licensee for residential purposes under the following mutual covenants:

1. DURATION:
The term of this licence is for 24 (twenty-four) months beginning from 1st November 2026 and ending on 31st October 2028.

2. COMPENSATION & ANNUAL ESCALATION:
The monthly compensation shall be Rs. 32,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Two Thousand only) for the first 12 months. Starting from the 13th month, there shall be an automatic annual rent escalation of 8% (eight percent) over the monthly compensation, amounting to Rs. 34,560/- per month for the second year.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND MANDATORY PAINTING CHARGES:
The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable deposit of Rs. 1,20,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Twenty Thousand only). The deposit shall be refunded within 30 (thirty) days from handing over possession, subject to a mandatory non-negotiable deduction of Rs. 15,000/- towards repainting and deep sanitization of the flat, regardless of the flat's handover condition or duration of stay.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD:
There shall be an initial lock-in period of 6 (six) months for both parties. Thereafter, either party may terminate this agreement by giving 1 (one) month's written notice.

5. APPLIANCE AND INFRASTRUCTURE MAINTENANCE:
The Licensee shall be exclusively responsible for all regular maintenance, servicing, and repairs of air conditioners, water purifiers, water heaters, and electrical fixtures. Furthermore, any extra water tanker fees levied by the society during summer months shall be paid directly by the Licensee.

6. SUNDAY INSPECTIONS:
The Licensor shall be permitted to enter and inspect the Premises on any Sunday between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM without prior appointment to check the upkeep of the flat or show the property to prospective tenants.

7. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION:
The full cost of stamp duty, electronic registration fees, and legal drafting charges shall be paid 100% by the Licensee.

8. SURRENDER:
The Licensee shall vacate and deliver peaceful possession upon completion or termination of this agreement.
