LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is made and executed at Mumbai on this 14th day of October 2026,

BETWEEN

MR. HARISH C. AGARWAL, residing at 1201, Imperial Heights, Lokhandwala Complex, Andheri (West), Mumbai - 400053 (the "LICENSOR");

AND

MR. AMITABH ROY, having permanent address at B-24, Salt Lake City, Sector 3, Kolkata - 700098 (the "LICENSEE").

WHEREAS the Licensor has agreed to license residential Flat No. 802, 8th Floor, 'Lotus Residency', Mahakali Caves Road, Andheri (East), Mumbai - 400093 (the "PREMISES") to the Licensee on the terms appearing hereunder:

1. DURATION:
The licence is granted for an 11-month term from 1st November 2026 to 30th September 2027.

2. COMPENSATION & AUTOMATIC REVISION:
The monthly compensation shall be Rs. 48,000/- (Rupees Forty-Eight Thousand only) payable on or before the 1st day of each month. In case the licence is extended beyond the initial term, there shall be an automatic 10% annual rent escalation over the current licence fee.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DELAYED REFUND:
The Licensee has deposited an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs only). The deposit shall be refunded within 90 days after the Licensee vacates the premises, and only after owner's complete satisfaction regarding the cleanliness and overall state of the flat. The Licensor retains the sole and final authority to deduct any amounts for restoration, whitewashing, and arbitrary damages without furnishing bills.

4. MANDATORY LOCK-IN PERIOD & PENALTY:
Both parties agreed to a strict mandatory 12-month lock-in period. If the Licensee terminates the agreement or vacates the Premises prior to the completion of the 12-month lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay a premature exit penalty equivalent to 3 months' rent, which the Licensor shall deduct directly from the security deposit.

5. STRUCTURAL AND ALL REPAIRS CHARGED TO LICENSEE:
The Licensee accepts the flat in "as-is" condition. The Licensee expressly agrees that all repairs charged to the licensee without exception, including all electrical, plumbing, drainage, internal seepages, wall dampness, and all major or structural repairs to the building fabric, shall be borne and paid for exclusively by the Licensee.

6. UNRESTRICTED INSPECTION RIGHTS:
The Licensor, his family members, and authorized agents shall have the right to enter upon and inspect the Premises at any time of the day or night without prior notice to the Licensee to verify the state of the property or exhibit the same to prospective buyers.

7. TERMINATION WITHOUT NOTICE:
The Licensor reserves the right to terminate this licence immediately with a 7-day notice if any condition is breached, whereas the Licensee cannot terminate during the lock-in period.
